CoreTechRoles is a job board for people who want to work in artificial intelligence and at technology companies. Before you rely on any job board, it helps to know what the data actually says about the current job market. To understand how we gather and categorize this data, you can read exactly [how we collect and label job listings](/blog/how-we-collect-and-label-job-listings).

We analyzed over 6,122 active open roles across 39 leading tech and AI companies—including OpenAI, Anthropic, Databricks, and Waymo—to see exactly what the AI job market looks like right now. 

Out of 6,122 total open roles, we identified **797 dedicated AI positions**. Here is what the data tells us about who is hiring, what they pay, and the skills they actually want.

## Research Roles Command the Highest Salaries

When it comes to compensation, pure AI research still dominates applied engineering. 

Looking at the median midpoint base salaries for open roles:
- **Research:** $370,000 (reaching as high as $850k)
- **AI Engineer:** $285,000
- **Machine Learning:** $276,000
- **Data Scientist:** $265,000
- **Forward Deployed:** $217,000

Seniority heavily dictates these numbers. The median midpoint for an Intern/Early Career role sits at $209,000, while Staff-level engineers command a median of $281,000, and Principal roles jump to $315,000.

## The Death of Remote AI Jobs?

If you are looking for a remote AI job, the odds are increasingly against you. 

Out of the 797 active AI roles we analyzed, **only 65 (about 8%) are listed as fully remote**. The remaining 732 require you to be in an office or on a hybrid schedule. 

The San Francisco Bay Area maintains a massive grip on the industry. San Francisco alone has 211 open AI roles, followed by Mountain View (128) and Sunnyvale (41). Outside the US, London is the strongest hub with 58 open positions.

Interestingly, those who *do* land remote AI roles aren't taking a pay cut. The median salary for a remote AI role is $276,000, compared to $256,000 for in-office roles. 

## The Most In-Demand AI Skills 

We parsed the job descriptions of all 797 AI roles to see which technologies are actually moving the needle. 

- **Python (465 mentions) & PyTorch (207 mentions)** remain the undisputed foundation of the AI stack, completely overshadowing TensorFlow (97) and JAX (81).
- **Evaluation (444 mentions)** is shockingly high. Companies are aggressively hiring engineers who know how to evaluate, benchmark, and test AI models, making it almost as in-demand as Python itself. 
- **LLMs (359 mentions) & Distributed Systems (254 mentions)** show that the focus has shifted from building small models to scaling and deploying massive foundational infrastructure.
- **RAG (60 mentions)** and **Fine-Tuning (123 mentions)** prove that applied, context-aware AI is becoming a core engineering requirement.

## Who is Hiring the Most?

Right now, the top AI labs are dominating the hiring volume. **OpenAI** currently lists 135 open AI roles, closely followed by **Anthropic** with 98. **Waymo** (91) and **Databricks** (78) are also making massive pushes for AI talent.
