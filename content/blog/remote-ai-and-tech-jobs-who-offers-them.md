## What "remote" means here

We label a listing as remote when the location says so explicitly, for example "Remote - United States", or when the job board marks the role as remote. We do **not** count "remote-friendly" roles that require an office or regular travel, and we don't count hybrid roles. That is stricter than some job boards, and it matters for a few employers (see below). The details are in [how we collect and label job listings](post:how-we-collect-and-label-job-listings).

## What the table shows

**Remote-first and remote-heavy are different things.** Reddit, ElevenLabs and Cohere post most of their roles as remote. Databricks has the fourth-most remote listings, but they are only 7% of its 883 openings. OpenAI has 22 remote listings out of 813. If you want a company where most of the team works remotely, look at the share, not the count.

**Several well-known employers have no fully remote listings in our data.** Anthropic, Scale AI, xAI, Figma, Wayve, Nuro, Figure AI, Perplexity and Fireworks AI all show zero. In [Anthropic](/companies/Anthropic)'s case, many listings say "Remote-Friendly (Travel-Required)", which we count as not fully remote. That wording can still suit some candidates, so read those postings rather than dismissing them.

**Remote AI-titled roles are a small slice.** 65 of the 797 listings with an AI or ML term in the title are remote, about 8%, close to the share across all listings. At least 30 of them are "Remote - United States", and 6 are "Remote - Ontario".

## Does remote pay more or less?

Among AI-titled listings that state pay, the median midpoint was about **$276K for remote roles (36 listings)** and about **$256K for non-remote roles (458 listings)**. We would not conclude anything from that. Thirty-six listings is a small sample, and it is dominated by a few employers. What we can say is that remote AI roles in this data are not obviously paid less. ## Reading a remote listing carefully

- **The place after the dash matters.** "Remote - United States" usually means you must live in the US. "Remote - Ontario" points to Canada. A remote role is often limited to certain countries or states.
- **Check time zones.** Some remote teams expect overlap with a headquarters time zone, even when the location line doesn't say so.
- **Ask how you would be employed.** Employers differ in whether they can hire in your country directly, use a contractor arrangement, or can't hire there at all. The posting may not say, so ask early.
- **"Remote" can change.** Policies shift, and a role listed as remote can later require some office time. Use the employer's page as the final word.

## What this data can't tell you

- It covers 39 companies, not the whole market. Plenty of remote AI jobs exist at companies we don't list.
- Our remote label depends on how each employer words its location, so a few roles that are remote in practice may not be counted.
- It's a snapshot. Remote roles open and close quickly.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many AI jobs are remote?**
Among listings with an AI or machine learning term in the title, 65 out of 797 (about 8%) were remote as of October 20, 2026. Across all 6,122 listings, about 9% were remote.

**Which companies hire the most remote workers?**
In our data, ElevenLabs, Reddit and Cohere have the highest share of remote roles (68% to 78%), and Databricks has the next-most remote listings (58).

**Is "remote-friendly" the same as remote?**
Not here. "Remote-friendly" roles that require travel or an office presence aren't counted as fully remote.

**Can I apply from any country?**
Often not. Many remote roles are limited to specific countries or regions. Check the location text and the employer's own page.
