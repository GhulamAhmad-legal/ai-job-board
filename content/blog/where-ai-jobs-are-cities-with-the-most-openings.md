## How we counted

For each active listing whose title includes a term such as AI, ML, machine learning, LLM, generative or data scientist, we took the **first location** the employer lists. A role listed in several cities is counted once, under its first city. We then grouped obvious spelling variants together (for example, "New York" and "New York City", or "Bengaluru" and "Bangalore"). Methodology is covered in [how we collect and label job listings](post:how-we-collect-and-label-job-listings).

## What the table shows

**The Bay Area dominates.** Add up San Francisco, Mountain View, Sunnyvale, San Jose and Palo Alto and you get 394 listings, about 49% of the total, and that is without counting smaller Bay Area towns that didn't reach the top of the table. Mountain View's number is likely helped by [Waymo](/companies/Waymo), which is headquartered there and has 91 AI-titled listings in our data.

**London is the largest hub outside the US.** At 58 listings it is bigger than Tokyo, Bengaluru and Singapore combined (48). Employers such as [Wayve](/companies/Wayve), which is UK-based, probably help explain it, but the listings alone don't show which employers account for how much.

**New York is the largest US hub outside the Bay Area.** Combining "New York" and "New York City" gives 39 listings, with another 9 shared with San Francisco.

**Asia and Europe appear in smaller clusters.** Bengaluru (19, counting both spellings), Tokyo (18), Singapore (11), Munich (10) and Seoul (6) each hold a modest number of listings. Because these numbers are small, a single employer opening an office can change the ranking quickly.

## Why concentration matters if you're job hunting

- **Location matters at the top of the market.** Outside the remote roles, a listing generally means working in or near that city, and relocation support varies. Check each posting.
- **Outside the hubs, you will see fewer roles.** If you live far from the cities above, the choice is between remote work (about 8% of AI-titled listings) and being open to relocation.
- **Look for employers expanding abroad.** Offices in London, Tokyo, Bengaluru, Singapore and Seoul show up in the data. Check whether the listing mentions visa or relocation support, which we don't track.

## What this data can't tell you

- **It reflects 39 companies.** Many are headquartered in the Bay Area, so the Bay Area looks bigger than it might in a broader sample.
- **First location only.** A role open in five cities counts in one.
- **City names are as employers write them.** We combined the obvious variants but may have missed some. "Washington" could mean the state or the capital, so we left it out.
- **It's a snapshot,** and employers open and close roles all the time.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are most AI jobs located?**
In our data, about half of AI-titled listings are in the San Francisco Bay Area, with San Francisco itself the largest at 211 listings. London is next among single cities at 58.

**Are there AI jobs outside the US?**
Yes. London, Bengaluru, Tokyo, Singapore, Munich and Seoul all appear in our top locations, along with a smaller number of remote roles tied to particular countries.

**Do I have to live where the job is?**
For most listings, yes, at least part of the time. Remote roles are about 8% of AI-titled listings, and they are often limited to certain countries.

**Will the ranking change?**
Probably, as employers expand or open new offices. This is a snapshot from October 20, 2026.
