**Short answer:** Safety, alignment and policy hiring is real but narrow, and it is mostly not research. Across **137 listings** whose titles match safety or policy keywords at 16 AI and tech companies (late October 2026), **[Anthropic](/companies/Anthropic) (55) and [OpenAI](/companies/OpenAI) (40) account for 95, or 69%**. Of the 128 that are about AI safety or policy, by our reading of the titles, about **18% are research, 27% are engineering or data science, 22% are policy and government affairs, 20% are operations and enforcement, and 13% are product, program management and communications.** The other 9 matched the keywords for other reasons, such as data governance.

## What the titles show

**Companies use different names for similar work.** Anthropic uses "Safeguards" as a team name: more than 30 of its titles contain it, across engineering, policy, product, data science and enforcement. OpenAI uses terms such as "Safety Systems" and "User Safety & Risk Operations." The consumer platforms (Discord, Reddit, Vercel, ElevenLabs, xAI) use "Safety" and "Trust & Safety" for abuse, content and child-safety work. Searching only for "AI safety" misses most of it.

**Research is a minority.** 23 of the 128 are research or research-engineering titles, spread across alignment, interpretability, agent safety, biological safety and safety training, mostly at OpenAI (12) and Anthropic (7).

**Engineering is the largest category, and often senior.** Seven Anthropic engineering titles begin with "Staff+", covering safeguards infrastructure, data, human review tooling and platform safety.

**Operations and enforcement roles are broad.** Anthropic lists ten "Safeguards Enforcement Analyst" roles, each focused on a different area of harm, such as child safety, cyber harm, bio harms, conventional weapons and account takeover. OpenAI lists several "User Safety & Risk Operations" roles, including one marked as a weekend shift, so some of this work follows a rota.

**Policy is its own track.** Titles such as "Model Policy Manager", "Policy Design Manager" and "Policy Development, Technical AI Governance" show policy work attached to specific products and risk areas. There are also government-affairs roles at Cohere and Harvey.

**There are some entry routes.** The list includes two "Anthropic Fellows Program" titles (one on AI safety and security, one on economics and policy). See also the [entry-level and internship roles](post:entry-level-and-internship-ai-tech-jobs-who-is-hiring), which include research fellowships at other companies.

## How to use this

- **Search by the employer's own words,** such as "safeguards", "trust and safety", "model policy", "interpretability" and "red team".
- **Decide which track you want.** Research, engineering, policy and operations ask for different backgrounds, and the share of research is small.
- **Read for seniority.** Many engineering titles are senior.
- **Start with the two large employers,** but check the smaller list too. Consumer platforms hire for safety at scale.

## What this data can't tell you

- **Titles only.** A safety role titled "Member of Technical Staff" or something generic isn't in our list, so the counts are a floor.
- **The categories are our reading** of the titles, not the employers' classification.
- It covers 39 companies and excludes vehicle-safety roles at Waymo, Nuro and Wayve.
- We did not measure pay for these roles.
- It is a snapshot from late October 2026.
