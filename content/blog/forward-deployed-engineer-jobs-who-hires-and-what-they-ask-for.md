## What a forward deployed engineer does

There is no single definition, but the descriptions in our data point the same way. A forward deployed engineer is an engineer who works directly with customers to get a product running in the customer's own setting. That usually means building integrations, adapting the product to the customer's data and workflows, and passing what they learn back to the product team. The title is generally associated with the data company Palantir, and AI companies now use it widely.

In our data, 199 of the 202 descriptions mention customers and 153 mention travel. This is a job where you spend a lot of time with people outside your own company.

## What we measured

We took every active listing whose title contains "forward deployed" or "FDE". We counted listings, distinct roles (so a role open in nine cities is one role), distinct titles and companies. We also read each description for a set of keywords, and for the stated pay range where there was one. A mention in a description is not the same as a requirement.

## If you are considering this path

- **Check how much you want customer contact.** Nearly every description mentions customers, and most mention travel.
- **Breadth helps.** Python and JavaScript or TypeScript both appear often, and SQL rarely.
- **Read the title for a sector.** At Databricks especially, the industry in the title shows who you would work with.
- **Look at neighboring titles** such as "Deployed Engineer" and "Solutions Architect" when you search.

## What this data can't tell you

- It covers 17 companies with a forward deployed title, out of the 39 we track. Other employers use the role under other names.
- Keyword matching is blunt: "travel" can appear in general company text, and a skill may be described without our exact word.
- Pay is available for 59% of listings, and an employer that doesn't publish pay may pay differently.
- It is a snapshot from late October 2026.
