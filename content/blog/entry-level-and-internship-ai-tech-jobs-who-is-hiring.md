## What we counted

We looked for titles containing intern, internship, new grad, new graduate, early career, graduate program, apprenticeship, residency, fellowship, student researcher or co-op, using whole-word matches. So a title like "International Sales Lead" does not count. Roles called "Junior", "Associate" or "Entry-level", and programs with other names, are not included. For example, Anthropic's "Fellows Program" listings don't use the word "fellowship," so this count misses them. The numbers below count distinct roles, not the same role posted in several cities.

## What the roles look like

We reviewed a sample of the titles. Here is what they show.

**Mostly for the 2027 cohort.** Most titles in the sample name a 2027 start, such as "Summer 2027", "Winter 2027" or "2027 Start", with a few for "2026 New Grad". Employers are recruiting well ahead of the start date.

**Engineering and research dominate, but not exclusively.** There are software engineering internships at Cohere, Databricks, Figma, Glean, Harvey and Replit, "Kernel Engineer - New Grad" and "Software Engineer - New Grad 2026" at Cerebras, and machine learning research internships at Cohere, Perplexity and Scale AI. There are also product management internships at Databricks, product design internships at Databricks and Figma, and a handful of non-engineering roles such as "Revenue Operations Intern" (Cohere), "Social Media Intern" (Nuro) and "Supply Chain Analytics Intern" (Figure AI).

**Hardware and robotics have many openings.** Figure AI's 12 intern roles cover mechanical, electrical, firmware, embedded software, hardware reliability, middleware and validation engineering, among others.

**There are PhD-level early-career roles.** Examples include "PhD GenAI Research Scientist Intern" (Databricks), "PhD Intern, AI Applied Scientist (2027)" (Figma) and "Member of Technical Staff, Systems Infrastructure (2026 PhD New Grad)" (Fireworks AI). There are also research programs such as the "Perplexity Research Fellowship" and "Research Internships" at Physical Intelligence. If you are weighing whether you need a doctorate for these roles, see our look at [what AI job descriptions say about PhDs](post:do-you-need-a-phd-for-ai-jobs).

**Some new-grad roles use the "Member of Technical Staff" title,** for example at Fireworks AI and Perplexity, which means the usual engineer and scientist labels won't find them.

## Why so few state pay

Only 20 of the 174 listings (11%) state pay in a form we can read, compared with roughly six in ten across all roles. Internship pay is often hourly or monthly, and our reader captures only annual ranges in US dollars, so the low figure largely reflects what we can read, not necessarily what employers publish. Check each posting for pay details.

Only 34 of the 174 (20%) carry the AI label. Most early-career roles at these companies are general engineering, product or operations roles whose titles don't contain AI terms.

## What this data can't tell you

- Pattern matching on titles misses programs with other names, and may include a few roles that are not really early-career.
- It covers 39 companies, mostly US-based, so it says little about other regions or smaller employers.
- It is a snapshot. Internship cycles open and close, so the counts will change.
- We don't see how competitive each role is.
